Draft, 19 October 2026. Nothing here is live. The eight industry pages and the About page are built only after you approve this copy. Mark changes on this file or reply with them, and they go into `content/industries.ts` and `content/about.ts`.

Every price below comes from the pricing module, so it changes if a price changes. Monthly prices are before 18% GST.

## Please check first

1. **Organic social posting (open input D4).** No page says we post on Instagram or Facebook for the business. Salons, restaurants, gyms and shops mention only Facebook and Instagram ads and Google profile posts. If Digital Growth includes organic posting, these four pages should say so.
2. **Proof (open input D5).** No client names, ratings, quotes or results appear anywhere, including About. Add them once real proof is chosen.
3. **Online ordering for restaurants.** The restaurant and shop FAQs say e-commerce and online ordering are not part of the plans (the pricing page lists them as not included). The homepage showcase shows Curry Cook as a "Restaurant with online ordering". Please confirm the FAQ answer is right.
4. **Suggested basket for clinics, coaching, home services and real estate.** The build plan suggests Website + Search for these, which is ₹27,999 a month plus GST. Say if you would rather suggest a smaller basket for some of them, for example home services.
5. **Page names.** Each page uses the label from the homepage industries line, so the retail page is called "Shops" and the clinics page "Clinics" (the page text mentions dentists too).
6. **Search phrases.** Titles and H1s target phrases such as "website for clinics" and "website for real estate agents". These were chosen by judgement, not keyword data.
7. **Thane West.** The About description and "Who we are" use "Thane West, Maharashtra", from the registered address.
8. **Cancellation wording (open input D3).** About's "Lock you in" uses the current placeholder: 30 days' notice, and your website, domain and content stay yours.
9. **The 5% sign-up discount** is mentioned in every "How much does it cost?" answer.
10. **Clinics.** The copy makes no claims about treatments or results, given the limits on how doctors may advertise in India. Worth a check if you have clinic clients with their own rules.
